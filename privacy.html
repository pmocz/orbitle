Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Orbitle (orbitle.app) is a free, browser-based logic puzzle game. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. We do not require registration or sign-in.

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage data, including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on site
  • General geographic region (country/city level)
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • How you arrived at the site (referral source)

This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you personally.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies to distinguish users and sessions. These are analytics-only cookies. Orbitle itself does not set any first-party cookies.

Local Storage

Your game progress (current puzzle state, statistics, and settings) is saved in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Third-Party Services

Orbitle uses Google Analytics. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Children's Privacy

Orbitle does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The game contains no user accounts, chat, or user-generated content.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
